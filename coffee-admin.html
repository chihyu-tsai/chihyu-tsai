Coffee journal editor

Your single place to add café links, confirm map details, and publish to the website.

All saved café details and coordinates are public. Your editor key is only sent to this website’s backend and is not stored in the browser.

This key works like a password: it lets only you edit the journal. The Terminal command copies it to your clipboard; paste it here with ⌘V. Visitors never need it.
